Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the virtual reality application “VR Distribution Prototype” (the “Application”), developed as part of an academic seminar project focused on evaluating and documenting deployment and distribution workflows for VR applications.

Data Collection and Usage

The Application does not intentionally collect, store, transmit, or share personally identifiable information from users.

The Application is intended solely for research and educational purposes.

While using the Application, certain technical information may automatically be processed by the Meta Quest platform or the operating system, such as:

  • Device information
  • Crash logs
  • Performance diagnostics
  • Platform account identifiers
  • Other technical telemetry handled by Meta Platforms Technologies

The developer of the Application does not directly access or store such information unless explicitly stated otherwise within the Application.

Third-Party Services

The Application may rely on platform services provided by Meta Platforms Technologies, LLC, including Oculus/Meta Quest services.

These services may process technical or operational data in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/

Children’s Privacy

The Application is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Data Security

No user accounts, personal databases, or cloud-based user profiles are maintained by the developer of the Application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future to reflect changes to the Application or project requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page.
